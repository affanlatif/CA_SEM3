# NOT Gate

## Aim
To implement a NOT gate using VHDL.

## Short Theory
A NOT gate is an inverter. It produces the complement of its single input: HIGH becomes LOW, and LOW becomes HIGH. The Boolean expression is Y = NOT A.

## Truth Table

| A | Y |
|---:|---:|
| 0 | 1 |
| 1 | 0 |
